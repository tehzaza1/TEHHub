    Mage's Legacy
    There are a number of possible Mage's Legacies, each granting a different bonus:
    • Legacy of Amethyst
    • Legacy of Basalt
    • Legacy of Bismuth
    • Legacy of Diamond
    • Legacy of Gold
    • Legacy of Granite
    • Legacy of Jade
    • Legacy of Quicksilver
    • Legacy of Ruby
    • Legacy of Sapphire
    • Legacy of Silver
    • Legacy of Stibnite
    • Legacy of Sulphur
    • Legacy of Topaz

    Only one instance of each Mage's Legacy can apply its bonus to you at a time.

    Legacy of Amethyst
    Legacy of Amethyst is a Mage's Legacy which grants +45% to Chaos Resistance.
    Legacy of Basalt
    Legacy of Basalt is a Mage's Legacy which grants 150% increased Armour.
    Legacy of Bismuth
    Legacy of Bismuth is a Mage's Legacy which grants +45% to all Elemental Resistances.
    Legacy of Diamond
    Legacy of Diamond is a Mage's Legacy which grants 75% increased Critical Hit Chance.
    Legacy of Gold
    Legacy of Gold is a Mage's Legacy which grants 45% increased Rarity of Items found.
    Legacy of Granite
    Legacy of Granite is a Mage's Legacy which grants +2000 to Armour.
    Legacy of Jade
    Legacy of Jade is a Mage's Legacy which grants +2000 to Evasion Rating.
    Legacy of Quicksilver
    Legacy of Quicksilver is a Mage's Legacy which grants 30% increased Movement Speed.
    Legacy of Ruby
    Legacy of Ruby is a Mage's Legacy which grants +60% to Fire Resistance and +5% to Maximum Fire Resistance.
    Legacy of Sapphire
    Legacy of Sapphire is a Mage's Legacy which grants +60% to Cold Resistance and +5% to Maximum Cold Resistance.
    Legacy of Silver
    Legacy of Silver is a Mage's Legacy which grants 30% increased Skill Speed.
    Legacy of Stibnite
    Legacy of Stibnite is a Mage's Legacy which grants 150% increased Evasion Rating.
    Legacy of Sulphur
    Legacy of Sulphur is a Mage's Legacy which grants 60% increased Damage, and makes the area within 2.5 metres of you Consecrated Ground while you're stationary.
    Legacy of Topaz
    Legacy of Topaz is a Mage's Legacy which grants +60% to Lightning Resistance and +5% to Maximum Lightning Resistance.
