An Audience With The King
An Audience with the King is an item that is an exclusive drop from the Ritual mechanic. This item allows you to access the Crux of Nothingness via the Realmgate.
Royal Tithe
Requires starting the Rite of the Nameless
SubTree: Ritual
15% increased progress towards an Audience With The King when offering Tribute
Prayer and Pledge
Requires starting the Rite of the Nameless
SubTree: Ritual
10% of Tribute spent also provides progress towards an Audience With The King
