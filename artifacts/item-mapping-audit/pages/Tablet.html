Precursor Towers Mods /90
Precursor Towers
Precursor Towers are ancient structures that are scattered all throughout the Atlas. Precursor Tower Maps can be completed to reveal a large area around them and to obtain a Tablet.

Completing a Precursor Tower requires you to activate the Precursor Beacon at the end of the Map, after defeating the Map Boss.
