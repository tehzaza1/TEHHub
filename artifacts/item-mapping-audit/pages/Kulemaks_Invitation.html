Kulemak's Invitation

Kulemak's Invitation is a special item that cannot be used directly. If the player visits The Well of Souls with this item in their inventory, a new option to "Jump In" to the well is made available. The result of doing so is currently unknown.

Item acquisition

Drops from Abyssal bosses.
