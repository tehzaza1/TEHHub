Supported Gem /9
  • Allow Type: Companion
  • Excluded Type: MinionsAreUndamagable
    • Rhoa Mount
    Harnesses a Companion Rhoa you can mount while you're wielding a Bow or Spear. While mounted you can use Bow, thrown Spear, and Mark Skills, and are much less slowed while using Skills, but being Hit will cause Heavy Stun buildup. While you aren't mounted, the Rhoa will attack your enemies alongside you with its beak, but can be damaged.
    Tame Beast
    Conjure Azmeri wisps to engulf a Rare Beast for a duration, Hindering them. If you defeat the Beast while it is engulfed in wisps, it will be captured by this gem, transforming the gem to instead allow you to summon the Beast as a Reviving Companion.
    Companion: {0}
    Summon a Reviving Beast Companion to aid you in combat.
    Azmerian Wolf
    Summons an ancient Reviving Azmerian Wolf that can howl, calling to spirits convening an Eternal Hunt on Command. It Maims when attacking foes, and can lunge to bite and inflict Bleeding, or howl to inspire nearby allies.
    Wolf Pack
    Activate to summon a pack of Wolf Companions. The pack treated as a single Companion for effects that count or limit Companions, regardless of the number of Wolves in the pack.
    Cackling Companions
    Activate to summon a cackle of Hyena Companions. The cackle is treated as a single Companion for effects that count or limit Companions, regardless of the number of Hyenas in the cackle. Hyenas whoop when they first appear, Hindering nearby enemies, and have a chance to Maim enemies they Hit.
    Summon Infernal Hound
    Activate to summon a Reviving Infernal Hound which Ignites enemies near it.
    Wild Protector
    Call upon a Bear Spirit Companion to maul your foes. The Bear Spirit leaps at enemies to Maim them, Leeches Life with its slam, and periodically roars to Intimidate enemies. The Bear Spirit does not count toward your Companion limit.
    Spirit Vessel
    Summons a Shapeshifting Spirit Vessel Companion that uses copies of socketed Bear, Werewolf, and Wyvern Skills.
